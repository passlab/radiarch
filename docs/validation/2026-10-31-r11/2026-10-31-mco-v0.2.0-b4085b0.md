## 4. Known limitations

- The Pareto plans are fluence plans (beamlet weights); navigation shows their blend. The deliverable plan is made afterwards by dose mimicking, so it can differ from the navigated plan: the deviation tables above measure by how much.
- The error bound is in the trade-offs normalized to ideal 0 and nadir 1 over the anchor plans, and it bounds the distance to the Pareto set of these beams and this dose matrix, not the quality of the dose engine (the engines' own records cover that).
- The error bound is approximate. It takes each converged plan as optimal, and a solve counts as converged when its cost stops falling (by at most 1e-5 of itself over 50 iterations), which does not prove optimality. Each plan records its Frank-Wolfe gap, but on fluence problems that gap is too loose to certify the bound; a certified bound is a follow-up.
- Robust sets: the scenarios are a few range and setup errors, not a probabilistic model; the smoothing of the worst case and the scenario-wise objective leave a gap in the bound (robust_gap) that more plans do not remove.
- Automatic navigation is radiarch's own method built from published parts; the commercial systems' methods are not published, so it is not compared with them here.
