## 4. Known limitations

- Generic 6 MV kernels and no linac head model: output factors, head scatter and off-axis softening are not modelled; the engine transports photons only, so the build-up is longer than a measured 6 MV beam.
- The shipped engine constants truncate scatter (dose cutoff 0.5 % of a beamlet's peak, 2 cm safety radius); radiarch's build takes both at run time (CCC_DOSE_CUTOFF, CCC_RSAFE_CM) and the 'full' setting removes the truncation at ~45x the cost. The comparison with the Monte Carlo reference (the kernels' own spectrum) is in the photon_vs_mc checks above; the full setting overshoots at depth, so the shipped setting stays the default for influence matrices and final doses.
- The Monte Carlo references share the engine's assumptions where the engine has them (water composition at the slab densities, a flat primary fluence collimated by jaws and MLC, no flattening filter), so they test transport, not the linac. They are step-converged (0.5 mm electron steps, 0.1 mm cut; a single and a split water box agree within 0.5 %).
