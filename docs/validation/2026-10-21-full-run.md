# Validation harness — first full run, 2026-10-21

Full mode (native grids, all primaries, every patient of each data case), on the
20-core workstation, radiarch commit `5e68386` plus the uncommitted R7a harness, before
the R14 driver; the proton case therefore ran through the vendored OpenTPS path and
against the Bragg–Kleeman fit (the PSTAR table and the 1 % tolerance were entered
after this run). Statuses are research targets, never a commissioning. The results
folder (`validation/results/`) is not committed; this file is its summary.

Reading of the three failures:

- `gamma_crosscheck_synthetic`: the 3 mm + 4 % perturbation scores 30.5 % against
  pymedphys's 55.8 % — the known gap, radiarch's gamma has no sub-voxel interpolation
  (TG-218 asks for ≤ 1/3 of the DTA). Scheduled for R5. The identical, 2 %-scaled and
  1 mm-shifted perturbations agree at 100 %.
- `geometry_volumes_lctsc`: 29 of 30 structure volumes within 5 % of the contour
  polygons; one esophagus (S2-102) is 5.0 % over — a thin structure on a 3 mm slice
  spacing, at the tolerance edge, not a defect.
- `dvh_crosscheck_pancreatic`: 47 of 50 graded checks agree with dicompyler-core; the
  three failures are listed below.
