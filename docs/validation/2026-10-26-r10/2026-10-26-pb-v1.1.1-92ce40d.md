## 4. Known limitations

- The kernel is a Monte Carlo pencil kernel of the Mohan 6 MV spectrum in water (the CCC kernels' spectrum), decomposed into a few depth-weighted components; there is no linac head model (no head scatter or off-axis softening), and the field edge is an ideal edge blurred by a Gaussian fluence penumbra fitted to the Monte Carlo reference.
- Heterogeneities change the radiological depth and the lateral spread: the kernel's part within 30 mm is stretched by the local density to the power -0.9. The two parameters were fitted to the Monte Carlo lung slabs (2 x 2 and 10 x 10 cm), so the lung checks are consistency checks; the photon scatter beyond 30 mm is not scaled and the build-down and re-build-up at interfaces are not modelled.
- The influence matrix stops 80 mm from each beamlet and thins its small entries by an unbiased roulette; final and segment doses use the full 200 mm kernel. Optimization doses therefore differ slightly from the final dose.
